BEHIND THE MAP

Local detail.
Statewide context.

This research adapts the Georgia Environmental Finance Authority’s framework to smaller geographic units using ACS 2024 five-year estimates from IPUMS NHGIS.

Read the research overview ↗
01 / INDICATORS

Ten dimensions of disadvantage

Income, employment, poverty, household assistance, age dependency, and population change contribute to the supplied index.

02 / SCORING

A common 10–40 scale

Nine indicators use geography-specific quartiles, with income scored in reverse. Population growth receives 1 point; decline up to 1%, up to 2%, and above 2% receives 2, 3, and 4 points.

03 / INTERPRETATION

A research screening measure

Scores exclude utility connection counts. They do not measure water bills, establish GEFA funding eligibility, or describe every household within an area. Tract and place thresholds differ.
