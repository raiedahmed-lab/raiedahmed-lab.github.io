I am **Raied Ahmed Nishat**, an **M.S. student in Civil Engineering and Graduate Research Assistant at Georgia Southern University**, working with [Dr. Ram Krishna Mazumder](https://scholars.georgiasouthern.edu/en/persons/ram-krishna-mazumder/).

My research combines machine learning and engineering analysis to assess the condition, resilience, and affordability of civil infrastructure. My current work focuses on automated sewer inspection, water distribution system performance, and water infrastructure affordability in Georgia. My background in geotechnical engineering includes published research on cross-regional soil strength prediction using transfer learning and on clay stabilization.

I am interested in a practical question: How can engineering data support more reliable infrastructure decisions when observations, resources, and local knowledge are limited?

## Current Research

### 1. Automated sewer condition assessment

Developing computer-vision methods to identify defects in sewer CCTV imagery and connect inspection results with asset management. The work aims to translate image-level predictions into useful condition information for rehabilitation planning.

**Focus:** Defect classification, condition assessment, and rehabilitation prioritization.  
**Status:** Model development and analysis in progress.

### 2. Water infrastructure affordability in Georgia

Investigating the spatial distribution of water infrastructure affordability using GEFA-based assessment and household income information. The work examines how geographic and socioeconomic variation can inform the identification of communities facing affordability challenges.

**Focus:** Affordability assessment, socioeconomic indicators, and spatial analysis.  
**Status:** Analysis in progress.

### 3. Water distribution system performance and resilience

Evaluating water networks using complementary measures of network structure, hydraulic performance, water security, and economic performance. This research supports a broader understanding of infrastructure reliability and priorities for improvement.

**Focus:** Network performance, risk assessment, and infrastructure asset management.  
**Status:** Analysis in progress.
